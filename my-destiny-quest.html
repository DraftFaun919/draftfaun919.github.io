✦ My Destiny Quest ✦

A Halloween-themed Adventure Map for Minecraft Bedrock Explore a spooky world as you fight to save Halloween! Experience new blocks, items, food, and eerie music while uncovering the mystery behind the stolen candy. Recommended for 1–4 players. v1.1 introduces the Haunted House Side Quest!

🕸️ Story

You begin your journey in a small town under attack by monsters. The villagers of the town entire candy supply has vanished. It’s up to you to track down the thief and save Halloween before it’s too late!

- Dark Merchant Shop – Trade Chocolate Coins for powerful gear. - Halloween Decorations, Weapons, and Armor - Hidden Cat Totems – Grant unique powers. Can you find them all? - Ranking System – Earn ranks from S+ to D based on completion time. - Over 90 Minutes of Gameplay! - Reward Code System – Unlock bonuses in this and my other adventure maps! - Pumpkin Charm – Guides players toward the next objective. - Mazes, Puzzles, and Parkour – A mix of challenges for all players. - Haunted House Side Quest – A brand new spooky mission! - Compact but Interconnected Map Design – Every area loops together seamlessly. - Skeletons that glow Halloween colors!
